Focus Under Attack

A short online game that lets participants experience how interruptions, notifications, prolonged sounds and intrusive micro-tasks affect concentration and performance.

Solve simple calculations. During the challenge, alternating distractions will appear: ink stains to remove, bubbles to pop, pop-ups, notifications, moving banners and sounds that continue even after you move to the next question.

Tip: ask everyone to play at the same time, then compare scores, feelings and focus-recovery strategies.

Rules

1. Solve as many calculations as possible.

2. Enter only the number and press Enter.

3. Some distractions must be completed before you can continue; others remain in the background.

4. At the end, you will receive a short report.
